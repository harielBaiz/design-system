# Avatar

A small visual that represents a person, a team or a bot.

<Primary />
<Controls of={AvatarStories.Default} />

## Usage

### When to use

- Show who wrote a message, owns an item or is in a list.
- Give a person a recognisable shape in dense lists and chat.
- Show presence (online, busy, away) next to a name.

### When not to use

- As a decorative image. Use a normal image.
- To show a logo or a product. Use an image or Icon.
- To show a count of people on its own. Use `Badge`.

### Best practices

- Always give the avatar a **name**. It builds the initials, the colour and the accessible name.
- Show a person's **photo** when you have one. The avatar falls back to initials if it fails to load.
- Pick a size that matches the text next to it: `sm` beside body text, `md` in lists, `lg` in headers.
- Use `AvatarGroup` for a stack. It collapses extra people into “+N”.
- Do not use presence colour alone to tell people something important. It is also read out.

## Accessibility

- The avatar is an image with an accessible name made of the **name** and the **presence**, for example “Ana Pérez, online”.
- The image is decorative (`alt=""`) because the name is already on the wrapper.
- Initials and icons are hidden from screen readers.
- The “+N” in a group is read as “N more”.
- Colours come from the status token pairs, which meet text contrast in all three themes.

## API

<Controls of={AvatarStories.Default} />

`AvatarGroup` takes `max` (default 4) and `size`. The “+N” avatar counts as one of the `max` slots.

## Tokens used

`--bg-muted`, `--border`, `--surface-{info,success,warning,danger}`, `--text-{info,success,warning,danger}`, `--border-{info,success,warning,danger}`, `--r-sm`, `--r-md`.
