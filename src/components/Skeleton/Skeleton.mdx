# Skeleton

A placeholder in the shape of content that is still loading.

<Primary />
<Controls of={SkeletonStories.Text} />

## Usage

### When to use

- Content takes **more than about a second** to load and you know its layout: lists, cards, profiles, tables.
- You want the page to stay stable, with no jump when content arrives.

### When not to use

- For a short wait. Show nothing.
- For a task with known progress. Use `ProgressCircle`.
- When you do not know the layout. Use a spinner (indeterminate `ProgressCircle`).
- For errors or empty data. Use `EmptyState`.

### Best practices

- Match the **size and position** of the real content. Skeletons that do not match make the page jump.
- Use text lines for text, a rectangle for images, a circle for avatars.
- Show the skeleton for the parts that are loading. Keep headings and labels you already have.
- Make the last line of a paragraph shorter, like real text.
- Do not show more than one shimmer per screen area. Calm beats busy.

## Accessibility

- Skeletons are **decorative** and carry `aria-hidden`. Screen readers would only hear noise.
- Mark the region that is loading with `aria-busy="true"` and remove it when done. Announce the finished result with a live region when it matters.
- The shimmer **stops** when the person prefers reduced motion. The grey shape stays.
- Do not use a skeleton as the only sign of loading for people who cannot see it: the busy state covers that.
