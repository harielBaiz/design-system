# Notice

An inline message that sits next to the content it is about.

<Primary />
<Controls of={NoticeStories.Default} />

## Usage

### When to use

- Explain a rule, a limit or a side effect near the place it applies.
- Warn about a problem in one section or one form.
- Confirm a result that stays visible on the page.

### When not to use

- For a message about the whole app. Use `Banner`.
- For a short confirmation that can disappear. Use `Toast`.
- For one field's error. Use the `messages` prop on the field.

### Best practices

- Write a short **title** that says what happened or what to know. Use the body for detail.
- Choose the tone by the effect on the person: **danger** blocks them, **warning** may hurt them, **info** helps them.
- Put at most two actions under the message. The first one is the safe one.
- Place the notice **before** the content it affects, so it is read first.
- Do not stack more than two notices in one place.

## Accessibility

- `warning` and `danger` use `role="alert"` and are read out when they appear. Other tones use `role="status"`.
- Meaning never relies on colour alone: each tone has its own icon and the title says what it is.
- The close button is named “Dismiss”. Change it with `dismissLabel`.
- When a notice appears after an action, do not move focus into it. The live region announces it.
